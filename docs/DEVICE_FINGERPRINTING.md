# Device Fingerprinting

Fingerprinting combines normalized hostnames, MAC/OUI vendor evidence, ports and services, SNMP descriptions, operating-system observations, banners, certificates, AD/CMDB correlation, and topology evidence. Rules deterministically classify infrastructure and hospitality equipment including servers, hypervisors, network devices, telephony, POS/PMS/IPTV, locks, CCTV, IoT, storage, virtual machines, and unknown devices.

Every result stores matched rules, hostname candidates, services, hardware, software, network, and certificate summaries. Administrators can override classification while original evidence remains available. Review states cover Needs Review, Automatically Identified, Partially Identified, Manually Verified, Ignored, False Positive, Duplicate, and Retired. Unknown OUI prefixes remain Unknown rather than being guessed.
