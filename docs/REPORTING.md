# Enterprise Reporting

Report templates retain HIOP Blue & Gold branding, parameters, category, and version. Reports contain ordered heading, KPI, chart, gauge, table, heat-map, and summary sections suitable for drag-and-drop clients. Executions preserve trigger, requester, format, row count, status, and output reference. Explicit schedules control frequency, timezone, recipients, format, and email delivery. PDF, XLSX, CSV, and printable HTML are supported.
