# Change Advisory Board

CAB meetings record schedule, timezone, quorum, members, attendance, agendas, votes, decisions, conditions, notes, and decision history. A voting user must be an active voting member, attendance must meet quorum, and recorded votes must support an approval decision. Conditional approvals retain their conditions. Deferred and rejected decisions do not approve an RFC.

The CAB workspace is available at `/changes/cab`. Admin mutations are enforced by the backend; all decisions are linked to the RFC and audit trail. HIOP does not generate votes or approvals.
