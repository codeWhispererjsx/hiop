# Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)

Corrective and Preventive plans belong to Problems and record a title, measurable objective, owner, due date, status, and approval. Tasks record assignments, due dates, completion, and verification state.

Independent verification records a passed, failed, or inconclusive result, evidence, verifier, and timestamp. Only a passed verification completes a task. Scheduled processing marks overdue tasks and supplies reminders; it never completes, approves, or verifies work.
