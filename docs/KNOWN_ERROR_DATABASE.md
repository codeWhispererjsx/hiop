# Known Error Database

Known Errors use `KE-YYYYMMDD-NNNNN` identifiers and belong to a Problem. They record symptoms, affected services/devices/properties, validated root cause, temporary workaround, permanent resolution, related Change and Knowledge records, status, version, timestamps, revisions, and attachments.

Lifecycle is `draft → approved → published → retired`. Administrator approval is required for publication and retirement. Published entries are searchable. Revisions preserve the reviewed snapshot.

Workarounds distinguish temporary and permanent instructions and capture verification, effectiveness (0–5), risk, required permissions, validation checklists, linked runbooks, linked SOPs, and approval state. Instructions are human-authored and never executed by the KEDB.
